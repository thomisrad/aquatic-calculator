Welcome to the OSLU Aquatic Chemical Calculator

    
This app helps you test water chemistry, calculate chemical dosages, and track readings over time across all 7 bodies of water at OSLU. All readings sync automatically across staff devices via GitHub.

  

  

    
Step 1

    
Select your body of water

    
Use the dropdown to choose which body of water you are testing. The volume is pre-set for each location.

Level 1 Indoor - Hot spa2,000 gal

      
Level 1 Indoor - Cold spa900 gal

      
Level 2 Outdoor - Pool47,000 gal

      
Level 2 Outdoor - Hot spa900 gal

      
Level 13 Outdoor - Pool33,000 gal

      
Level 13 Outdoor - Hot tub3,500 gal

    

  

  

    
Step 2

    
Enter your ORP controller readings

    
Enter the current ORP probe reading and the controller setpoint in millivolts (mV). These come directly from your ORP controller display.

    
Tip: If ORP is well below setpoint, check chemical supply level, probe condition, and pH (high pH suppresses ORP).

  

  

    
Step 3

    
Enter your water chemistry readings

    
Fill in your test kit results. Only pH and free chlorine are required. Total alkalinity, calcium hardness, and temperature are also needed for the LSI calculation.

    

      
ParameterTarget range

      
pH7.2 - 8.0 (city req)

      
Free chlorine1.5 - 10.0 ppm (city req)

      
Combined chlorineLess than 50% of FC (city req)

      
Total alkalinity60 - 160 ppm (city req)

      
Calcium hardness200 - 400 ppm (pools), 150 ppm (spas)

      
Cyanuric acidOutdoor pools: target 75 ppm (range 30-90) | Outdoor spas: target 30 ppm (max 90) | Indoor: not applicable

      
TemperatureCurrent water temp in F

    

  

  

    
Step 4

    
Calculate dosages

    
Select "Calculate now" from the dropdown. The app shows ORP status, LSI balance, and exact chemical dosages.

    
A [SHOCK] red card means combined chlorine is too high -- follow the dose and retest after 4 hours of circulation.

History and Trends tabs

    
History shows all shared readings from all staff, filterable by body of water. Trends charts any parameter over time. Use Refresh in History Actions to pull the latest data from GitHub.

  

  

    
Important reminders

    
Always add chemicals with the pump running and allow full circulation before retesting. Sodium hypochlorite (12.5%) degrades over time -- check concentration before dosing. Never mix chemicals directly.
